## 3. People, voices and consent

IV Studio lets you build characters, voices and swaps. Only use:

- invented people,
- yourself, or
- someone who has agreed, and who can take that agreement back.

IV Studio asks you to confirm this before it trains a character, makes a RefMod or saves a voice from a recording. Don't
tick that box unless it's true. Keep proof of consent for anyone real.

## 5. Reporting misuse

If you see something made with IV Studio that breaks these terms, or that uses you or someone else without consent,
report it to **[abuse@infinitevariance.ai](mailto:abuse@infinitevariance.ai)** or with **Settings › About › Report
misuse**. Include links or files if you can. We read every report, investigate it promptly, and take the steps we can:
removing anything we host or distribute, refusing further access to services we run, and passing reports of illegal
content to the platform that hosts it and to the authorities. How reports are handled is set out in
[docs/SAFEGUARDS.md](docs/SAFEGUARDS.md).
